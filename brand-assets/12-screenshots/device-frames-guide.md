# Screenshot & Device Frames Guidelines

## 1. Google Play Store Specifications
- **App Icon**: 512 × 512 px, 32-bit PNG (with alpha). Max file size: 1024KB.
- **Feature Graphic**: 1024 × 500 px, JPG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).
- **Phone Screenshots**:
  - Minimum 4 screenshots, Maximum 8 screenshots.
  - Recommended dimensions: 1080 × 1920 px (16:9) or 1080 × 2400 px (20:9).
  - Format: 24-bit PNG or JPEG.

## 2. Apple App Store Specifications
- **6.9" Display (iPhone 16 Pro Max)**: 1320 × 2868 px.
- **6.7" Display (iPhone 15 Pro Max)**: 1290 × 2796 px.
- **6.5" Display (iPhone 14 Plus)**: 1284 × 2778 px.
- **13" iPad Pro**: 2064 × 2752 px.

## 3. Best Practices
1. Include high-contrast captions using brand typography (Poppins 900 Ultra-Bold).
2. Showcase core tools directly: AI Background Remover, Passport Photo Maker, PDF Merger.
3. Keep essential UI inside the safe zone (padding 60px from top and bottom).

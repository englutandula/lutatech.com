# Universal Brand Asset Specification

This document details the exact manifest of all master visual assets created for LutaTech.

## Package Structure Overview
- `01-logo/`: Full suite of primary, horizontal, stacked, icon-only, light, dark, and monochrome vector logos + PNGs (512 to 2048px) with Poppins 900 Ultra-Bold Geometric typography and flat-top chevron icon mark.
- `02-favicon/`: Multi-resolution `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48, 64), vector `favicon.svg`, individual PNGs, and Apple Touch icon (180x180).
- `03-app-icons/`: Full Android icon package (mipmaps: mdpi, hdpi, xhdpi, xxhdpi, xxxhdpi, adaptive foreground/background, notification) and iOS AppIcon suite (20px to 1024px).
- `04-social/`: Profile pictures for all major social networks, high-res banners/covers, and OpenGraph/Twitter card images.
- `05-patterns/`: Repeatable vector brand patterns for digital and print.
- `06-backgrounds/`: Ultra HD gradient and subtle background wallpapers.
- `07-icons/`: Consistent 24px UI icon system in SVG.
- `08-illustrations/`: Modern 404, empty-state, and status illustrations.
- `09-images/`: Google Play Store feature graphic (1024x500).
- `10-colors/`: Machine-readable tokens (`palette.json`, `colors.css`) in Adobe Red (#EB1000) and Dark (#090B10).
- `11-typography/`: Typography scale definitions (`typography.json`, `typography.css`) with Poppins 900 Ultra-Bold.
- `12-screenshots/`: Mobile store frame specifications and guidelines.
- `13-print/`: Print-ready vector business cards, letterheads, and envelopes.
- `14-presentations/`: 16:9 presentation slide templates.
- `15-brand-guidelines/`: Complete design system manual.
